## The knobs

    -S s1,s2   override minsync1 / minsync2, negative leaves stock
    -j n       DT jitter step iifac (stock 8)
    -Y n       number of passes (stock 3; extras repeat the final pass on the
               residual after another round of subtraction)
    -N 15..20  append per-symbol-SNR-weighted demodulator trials after the
               stock 14; first success still wins, so they only ever add
    -y a, -U n research overrides for the weighting exponent and window

The weighting idea is the MRC experiment from 2026-08-26 (`src/wsprd-mrc`),
which measured +12..13% decodes on slowly fading channels when the weighting
REPLACED the stock soft symbols, but -11% on AWGN and -5% at 1 Hz spread, and
so was parked.  The fix is structural: run the weighted demodulations as six
EXTRA trials (noncoherent blocks 1,2,3 and coherent 27,9,5 at exponent 0.5)
after the stock fourteen.  The trial loop keeps the first success, so channels
where weighting hurts cannot lose anything.

## Decisions

SHIP, as the new Deeper argument set -N 20 -S 0,-1 -Y 4 on top of the existing
-C 500 -o 4 -d -n a -X 1 -A:

  - the extra pass (-Y 4): +24 and +16 spots with zero losses and zero
    fabrications; the simulator agrees (+90 spots/10000 tx on a crowded band)
  - the weighted trials (-N 20): +1 and +12 off-air, +15% decodes (+0.35 dB)
    at threshold on a slowly fading simulated path, structurally incapable of
    losing a decode, no false decodes anywhere
  - the open refinement gate (-S 0,-1): +11/-4 and +4/-1 off-air, +13%
    decodes (+0.15 dB) at threshold in the simulator, no false decodes in this
    round; the earlier ms1/syncsafe campaigns saw about 1 in 3000 files
  - combined, off-air: +26 and +30 net spots per 177 slots (about +1%),
    zero fabrications on either band

DO NOT SHIP the finer DT jitter (-j).  Without -A it strictly loses decodes
(more offsets means more chances to hit an unpackable OSD result, which stock
abandons the setting for).  With -A, off-air, it found the most spots of any
single knob (+35/-4 on 20 m) but also produced the campaign's only fabricated
callsign, 45E/3U4KAB 27, which is exactly the more-attempts failure mode the
GLRT study predicted.  The combo gets comparable gains without it.

## The blanker-sweep fabrications, investigated (2026-08-27 evening)

Reproduced deterministically: decode corpus20 slots 1514,1516,1518 in order
with the tip build and full sweep arguments, and CO9ECR IM08 / OG0OCC HJ39
appear exactly as in the validation run, twice each, second pass, sync ~0.24,
one of the four by Fano and one by OSD, 28..44 of 162 hard symbol errors.

Mechanism.  On a clean band the strongest samples ARE the strong signals, so
the blanker mutilates every transmission at once.  Slot 1516 has a wall of
strong signals around 1540..1590 Hz (DG1GHR at +6 dB, GM7FIE at +2, OE3KFB,
IU0JJD, DL0T...).  Sweeping the fixed settings one at a time shows the ghost
appears at EVERY blanking level from 6% up (2 copies at 6, 10, 15 and 20%)
and never at 0 or 3%, while the slot's real decode count falls 17 -> 15 -> 12.
The sweep keeps the union of every setting, so the ghosts always ship.
nhardmin does not separate them: ghosts sit at 28..44 and real decodes under
blanking reach 30+ (one corroborated decode at 57).

Why three days of testing never saw it: the blanker's false-decode tests were
single-signal files (clean and QRN) and a busy band with HEAVY QRN, and in all
of those it really is safe; the failing case needs a CLEAN band crowded with
STRONG signals.  And the 2026-08-26 off-air A/B was run without -n a: its arm
B spot set (3638 corroborated, 16 others, all real calls) matches this
campaign's no-blanker base arm exactly, not the with-blanker run (3675 + 23).
Tonight's validation was the first time the sweep ever met a clean crowded
band.

The trade as measured on 20 m, 177 slots: the sweep buys +37 corroborated
spots (extra attempt diversity) and costs ~6 fabricated calls and ~6x CPU.

Candidate fixes, unbuilt:
  a. impulsiveness gate: skip the sweep when the sample distribution is
     Gaussian.  Kills the ghosts and the 6x CPU on quiet bands, but forfeits
     the +37 attempt-diversity spots.
  b. subtract-first: run the nb=0 decode to completion, subtract everything
     found, then sweep the blanker over the residual.  The mechanism needs
     strong signals to mangle; after subtraction there are none, while QRN
     impulses remain for the blanker to remove.  Keeps the attempt-diversity
     upside; costs nothing on CPU (later passes see an emptier band).
  c. re-verify blanker-only decodes against the UNBLANKED samples: a ghost
     exists only in the mangled data.  Needs care near the code's limit (the
     GLRT study showed wrong-by-50-symbol codewords can score close to truth
     at threshold SNR).
Preferred: (b), measured against (a) on both corpuses plus simulated QRN.

## Round three, 2026-08-27 late: fixing the sweep, and recall (in progress)

Three builds, all from the committed Max tip (byte-identical with their
features off):

  src/wsprd-nb3  subtract-first residual blanking: the sweep reads the file
                 once, and each blanked attempt restores the pristine buffer,
                 subtracts everything already decoded, and blanks the strongest
                 samples of the residual at baseband.
  src/wsprd-nb5  out-of-band impulse detection: the detection stream is the
                 full recording minus 1200..1800 Hz (and outside 300..5500),
                 which impulses dominate and WSPR signals cannot reach; raw
                 samples are blanked at full 12 kHz resolution where that
                 stream exceeds k sigma, k swept {8,6,5,4,3} instead of a
                 percentage.  File read once, reprocessed per setting.
  src/wsprd-rv   return-visitor recall (-r): wspr_priors.txt remembers every
                 decode's frequency, DT and drift for 30 minutes (per dial
                 frequency, max 60); at full depth each fresh prior is
                 injected as a candidate (sync forced high) unless a normal
                 candidate or a decode already sits within 0.5 Hz.  Acceptance
                 unchanged: Fano or hash-gated OSD must still pass.

percentile blanker (subtract AFTER blanking) and the ghosts survived intact,

intermodulation residue, which subtracting the clean signal estimate cannot
reach.  Order is everything.

The fabrication taxonomy, now measured across every campaign: the raw
percentile sweep mutilates strong signals and fabricates type-1 messages
(fixed architecturally by detection-aimed blanking); every OTHER fabrication
in every arm is a BARE TYPE-2 (prefixed or suffixed callsign, no brackets)
from the FANO path with nhardmin >= 30, sync <= 0.15, negative metric, on the
extra passes.  Real bare type-2s (42 per 177 slots on 20 m, all corroborated)
decode with nhardmin 9..20 or via hash-gated OSD.  Hence -G: reject bare
type-2 Fano decodes with nhardmin > 24 before subtraction and print.  On the
first 40 slots the guard removes exactly the two fabrications there and
nothing else.  One fabrication (3R2BOP/3) still passed on the full corpus;
full-corpus row capture running to set the threshold on evidence.

## The guard, completed (2026-08-28 morning)

50% threshold moves from about -33.1 to about -35.3: some 2.2 dB for stations
heard in the last half hour, all through the ordinary acceptance chain.  The
rvgain2 run that showed 250/250 at -37 was measuring a trap, not a triumph:
the cache still held the STRONG seeding cycle, so "stacking" was re-decoding
the previous transmission, which in deployment would report a spot for a
cycle in which the station may not even have transmitted.  Hence the honesty
rule in wsprd-rv3: a cached cycle may only be stacked if it is strictly newer
than the prior's last decode, so a transmission that was already reported (or
was strong enough to have been) can never masquerade as a new one.

wsprd-rv3 = ship4 + recall + stacking with: at most 20 priors injected, only
on the first full-depth pass, 5 DT offsets instead of 17 for injected
candidates, the honesty rule, and no stacked bare type-2 Fano accepts.

## Recall, landed (2026-08-28 afternoon)

The first off-air recall run (wsprd-rv4) added +1 corroborated on 20 m and +4
on 30 m but fabricated one type-1 callsign (453ZNJ BE67) through plain Fano
acceptance on an injected candidate.  The fix follows from what a prior IS: a
claim that a KNOWN station is transmitting, and known stations are in the hash
table by construction, so wsprd-rv5 accepts an injected candidate's decode
ONLY through the hash-gated deep search.  That gate costs nothing measurable:
the simulator gain curve is identical point for point (50% at about -35.1 dB,
2 dB beyond the known-station limit), rvfalse stays at zero (1800 warm-prior
noise files across rounds), and off-air:

    20 m   ship4 3737/24/0  ->  rv5 3738/26/0   (+1 slot, +2 day, clean)
    30 m   ship4 2671/ 3/0  ->  rv5 2675/ 3/0   (+4 slot, clean)

The corpus understates recall's value: its stations rarely linger in the
-33..-35 window where the 2 dB lives.  On fading night-time paths the sim
curve is the better guide.
